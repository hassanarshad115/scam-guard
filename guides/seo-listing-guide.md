## Ranking tips (sabse important)

1. **Icons & screenshots zaroor bhejein** - Chrome ranking me visuals bhi ginate hain.
   `assets/store/` me ready screenshots hain.
2. **Category**: Chrome me "Productivity" ya "Security" select karein. Security category
   kam crowded hai aur search me behtar nazar aati hai.
3. **Publish hone ke 2-4 hafte tak har 2 din apne contacts/friends se install karwayein**
   aur genuine reviews lein. Naye extensions jo ratings na hon, wo niche dalte hain.
4. **Rating 4.5+ rakhein** - kisi masle par user ko jaldi jawab dein (support email zaroor
   dein, kyunki Google support channel poochta hai).
5. **Updates regular karein** - mahine me ek update ranking signal hota hai.
6. **Short description me pehle 3 words sabse important hain** - "Detects fake phishing..."
   rakha gaya hai.
7. Pehla year realistic ho: 3-6 mahine me 1,000-5,000 installs aana normal hai agar
   niche theek hai.
