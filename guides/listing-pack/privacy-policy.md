# Privacy Policy - Scam Guard

_Effective date: (apni publish ki date likhein)_

## Summary

Scam Guard is a phishing and fake website detector browser extension. It is built
on a simple principle: **your data is your own**. Scam Guard collects nothing,
stores nothing on any server, and shares nothing.

## What data does Scam Guard collect?

**None.**

Scam Guard has no account system, no analytics, no trackers and no remote servers.
The extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data,
browsing history, passwords, payment information or any other user data.

## Where does detection happen?

All website detection runs **100% inside your browser**, on your own device.
When you visit a website, Scam Guard reads only the web address (URL) of the
current tab and checks it locally against scam-detection rules that ship with
the extension. No URL, no page content and no browsing history is ever sent to
any server, because there is no server.

## What about the blocked and trusted lists?

Your blocked and trusted site lists are stored **only in your own browser's
local storage**. They are never uploaded. You can export them to a file or
import them back at any time, and you can delete them at any time.

## Does Scam Guard change or modify my data?

No. Scam Guard never modifies, reads the content of, or sends any of your data.
It only shows warnings about suspicious websites and remembers your own
block/trust choices in your browser.

## Third-party services

Scam Guard does not integrate with any third-party service, analytics provider,
ad network or data broker.

## Children's privacy

Scam Guard is a general-purpose privacy tool and does not knowingly collect any
personal information from anyone, including children.
