# Privacy Policy - Scam Guard

_Effective date: August 9, 2026_

## Summary

Scam Guard is a phishing and fake website detector browser extension. It is built
on a simple principle: **your data is your own**. Scam Guard does not collect or
upload any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics and no trackers.

## What data does Scam Guard collect?

**None.**

Scam Guard does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data, browsing
history, passwords, payment information or any other user data. It has no
account system, no analytics, no trackers and no data brokers.

## Where does detection happen?

Detection runs **inside your browser**, on your own device. When you visit a
website, Scam Guard reads the web address (URL) of the current tab and checks it
locally against scam-detection rules that ship with the extension. On the page
itself it also inspects page structure locally - links, login forms and input
fields - so it can warn you before you type a password or card number into a
suspicious page. This inspection is local: the page content is never sent
anywhere.

## The live phishing feed (the only remote download)

A couple of times a day, Scam Guard downloads a **public list of reported scam
and phishing domain names** from the CERT.PL abuse feed (a Polish research and
academic network's cybersecurity team, https://hole.cert.pl). This download is
**domain names only** - it contains no URLs, no page content, and nothing about
you or your browsing is included or sent. The download happens automatically to
keep the protection current, and you can **turn it off at any time** in the
extension settings. Legitimate domains (brand websites and the roots of shared
hosting platforms) are filtered out and can never be blocked by this feed.

## What about the blocked and trusted lists?

Your blocked and trusted site lists are stored **only in your own browser's
local storage**. They are never uploaded. You can export them to a file or
import them back at any time, and you can delete them at any time.

## Does Scam Guard change or modify my data?

No. Scam Guard never modifies or uploads your data. It only shows warnings about
suspicious websites and remembers your own block/trust choices in your browser.

## Third-party services

The only third-party connection is the twice-a-day download of the public phishing
domain list described above. Scam Guard does not integrate with any analytics
provider, ad network or data broker, and no personal data is ever shared with
any party.

## Children's privacy

Scam Guard is a general-purpose privacy tool and does not knowingly collect any
personal information from anyone, including children.
