Scam Guard is a free phishing protection and scam detector that blocks fake websites, dangerous links and online fraud BEFORE you enter your password, card number or personal information.

HOW IT PROTECTS YOU (phishing protection features)
- Blocks fake websites and fake login pages the moment you open them
- Warns about lookalike websites that copy PayPal, Google, Amazon, Netflix, your bank and 150+ other brands
- Flags dangerous links in Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo search results
- Shows you the safety verdict on every link before you click it (just hover)
- Stops you pasting your password into suspicious pages
- Warns when a login page sends your password to another website
- Warns about fake crypto wallets (MetaMask, Ledger, Binance and more)
- Catches scam links hidden behind short links like bit.ly and adf.ly
- Live scam list updated every day catches brand-new fakes

WHY PEOPLE LOVE IT
- 100% free - no account, no subscription, no limits
- 100% private - everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded
- Easy English - simple warnings you understand in one second
- Fast and lightweight - uses almost no memory
- One click to block or trust any website
- Works on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera and Brave

WHO IT IS FOR
Scam Guard is for everyone who uses the internet - students, parents, grandparents, online shoppers and anyone with a bank account. If a scammer tries to trick you, Scam Guard stops them.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is Scam Guard really free?
Yes. Scam Guard is 100% free with every feature included. There is no premium version, no trial, no account and no subscription.

Is Scam Guard safe to use?
Yes. Scam Guard never collects or uploads your data. All detection runs inside your browser. The only download is a daily public list of scam domains - nothing personal is ever sent.

Does Scam Guard sell my data?
No. There is no analytics, no trackers, no ad network and no data broker anywhere in the extension.

Will Scam Guard slow down my browser?
No. Scam Guard is lightweight and uses almost no memory.

Can Scam Guard block a website by mistake?
If a safe website is ever flagged, one click adds it to your Trusted Sites list and it will never be warned about again.

9) STORE LISTING > Permissions justification (mandatory)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
storage - Saves your settings, sensitivity level and your blocked/trusted site lists. Everything is stored only in your own browser; nothing is uploaded.

tabs - Reads the web address (URL) of the current tab only, so Scam Guard can check whether the site you are visiting is a phishing or fake website. It never reads page content, passwords or personal data.

alarms - Schedules the once-a-day download of the public phishing domain list so it stays up to date.

contextMenus - Adds the "Check this link with Scam Guard" item when you right-click a link, so you can verify a URL before opening it.

notifications - Shows a one-time result notification after you use "Check this link", so you see whether the link is safe without leaving the page.

Read and change all websites' data (<all_urls>) - Lets the extension check the URL of every website you open so it can show a full-screen warning BEFORE you enter your password on a fake site. All detection runs locally inside the browser; no data is sent to any server.
