3) Long description

Protect yourself from online fraud with Scam Guard - a free, private phishing and fake website detector that warns you BEFORE you enter your password, card number or personal information.

HOW IT WORKS
Scam Guard scans every website you visit inside your browser and looks for the tricks scammers use every day:
- Lookalike brand domains (paypal-secure-login.xyz)
- Typo and leet-speak domains (paypa1.com, g00gle.com, amaz0n.com)
- Suspicious new domains (.xyz, .top, .icu and more)
- Non-English lookalike (homograph) characters and punycode
- Classic URL tricks like the "@" symbol and IP addresses
- Login forms that secretly send your password to ANOTHER domain
- Password fields on unencrypted (HTTP) pages
- Live blacklist: reported phishing domains from OpenPhish, URLhaus and cert.pl, refreshed daily

When a fake site is found, a full-screen warning blocks the page and tells you exactly why it looks suspicious - before you type anything. You can choose a lighter corner warning instead.

EXTRA PROTECTION, ALL FREE
- Toolbar icon shows a live checkmark (safe) or warning (!) for every site
- Risky links in Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo results are flagged before you click
- Hover any link to see its safety verdict instantly
- Right-click any link and choose "Check this link with Scam Guard"
- Paste guard stops your password being pasted into suspicious pages
- One-click block or trust any site

WHAT MAKES SCAM GUARD DIFFERENT
- 100% free - no account, no subscription, no limits
- Private - your URLs, passwords and history never leave your browser; the only download is a daily public list of scam domains
- Works on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera and Brave
- Fast and lightweight - uses almost no memory
- Easy to use - one-click block or trust any site

KEEP YOUR PASSWORDS SAFE
Online scams and phishing attacks are growing every year. Scam Guard gives everyone free protection from fake websites, whether you are checking your bank, buying online or just browsing.

5) Permissions justification (Edge puchta hai)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
storage - Saves your settings, sensitivity level, your blocked/trusted site lists and your local reports. Everything is stored only in your own browser; nothing is uploaded.

alarms - Schedules the once-a-day download of the public phishing domain list so it stays up to date.

contextMenus - Adds the "Check this link with Scam Guard" item when you right-click a link, so you can verify a URL before opening it.

notifications - Shows a one-time result notification after you use "Check this link", so you see whether the link is safe without leaving the page.

Read all websites' data - Reads the web address (URL) of the page you are visiting so Scam Guard can check whether it is a phishing or fake website, and show a warning before you enter your password. Detection runs locally; no data is sent anywhere.
