Privacy Policy - Scam Guard

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Summary

Scam Guard is a phishing and fake website detector browser extension. It is built on a simple principle: your data is your own. Scam Guard collects nothing, stores nothing on any server, and shares nothing.

What data does Scam Guard collect?

None.

Scam Guard has no account system, no analytics, no trackers and no remote servers. The extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data, browsing history, passwords, payment information or any other user data.

Where does detection happen?

Website checking runs inside your browser, on your own device. When you visit a website, Scam Guard reads only the web address (URL) of the current tab and checks it locally against scam-detection rules that ship with the extension. No URL, no page content and no browsing history is ever sent to any server.

The live phishing feed

Once a day, Scam Guard downloads a public list of reported scam and phishing domains from an open-source repository (served through a content delivery network). This download contains only domain names from public blacklists such as OpenPhish, URLhaus and cert.pl - it contains no personal data, and nothing about you or your browsing is included or sent. You can turn this feed off at any time in the extension settings.

What about the blocked and trusted lists?

Your blocked and trusted site lists are stored only in your own browser's local storage. They are never uploaded. You can export them to a file or import them back at any time, and you can delete them at any time.

Does Scam Guard change or modify my data?

No. Scam Guard never modifies, reads the content of, or sends any of your data. It only shows warnings about suspicious websites and remembers your own block/trust choices in your browser.

Third-party services

The only third-party connection is the daily download of the public phishing domain list described above. Scam Guard does not integrate with any analytics provider, ad network or data broker, and no personal data is ever shared with any party.

Children's privacy

Scam Guard is a general-purpose privacy tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
