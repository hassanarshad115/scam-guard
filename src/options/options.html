How to use Scam Guard

  1. Pin Scam Guard to your toolbar - click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions) at the top right, then the pin next to the Scam Guard shield. Now the shield always stays visible.
  2. Just open any website - Scam Guard works automatically. A small popup shows the verdict: green check = safe, red warning = dangerous. No clicks needed.
  3. Check links before you click - hover a link to see its verdict, or right-click a link and choose "Check this link with Scam Guard".
  4. You are in control - click the shield icon to block or trust any site. Blocked = always stopped, trusted = never warned.
